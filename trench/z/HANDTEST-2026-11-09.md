### Where it comes from, and the fix

- **Introducing PR (high confidence, from the log):** #1000 (LAT14). It is the change that turned netflix's frames from 908 full relayouts into 2,626 paint-only frames. The render path those frames take is LAT2's (#941) paint-only frame on LAT13's (#992) deferred renderer. Netflix had never run that combination in a hand test, because Pete's last netflix visit predates both #992 and #1000.
- **Code path (medium-low confidence until the real-window frames are compared):** `Engine::paint_animation_frame` (`lib.rs:10671`, patch + `display_list_for` + `render` per frame) → `Renderer::execute_deferred_layers` / `place_layer` (`rustkit-renderer/src/lib.rs:2226`, `place_layer` `:2354`, `pixel_rect` `:7233`). In the deferred path, an animated group's composite rect (`pixel_rect` of the group's bounds) changes every frame. When the 4 slots per depth overflow, the frame drops back to the pass-per-layer path (`abandon_deferred_frame` → `Ok(false)`). A page with an animated group near the slot limit can switch paths from frame to frame. Text inside a group that is resampled at a different sub-pixel origin each frame would shimmer while the page itself does not move. My 1x fixture does not trigger this; netflix's real tree (5 animated elements, many nested groups, a Retina backing) is the open variable.
- **Fix:** snap each deferred layer's composite rect and the animated layer's origin to whole device pixels, and keep one path per page rather than per frame, so text inside an animated or faded group is drawn at the same device-pixel phase every frame. Until that lands, `RUSTKIT_ANIMATION_REPAINT=0` restores the old (slow) full-relayout frames for an A/B on Pete's machine.

## 2. eBay images not loading (long-standing; LAT9 / S4)

### The failure, with counts (eBay search, 18:05:25 to 18:06:16)

- **84 images loaded during the load** (`Loaded images count=84`, 18:05:37): 79 of them are `s-l140.webp` result thumbnails.
- **78 images dropped, 0 loaded** after the load: at 18:06:10.43, 78 `s-l500.webp` requests log `Loading image` and then `Image over the subresource budget; rendering without it` **within 0.1 ms each**. The fetch is never made. In the same burst, 1 SVG fails over budget and 1 tracking pixel is blocked by the shield.
- **Why the URLs change:** the server HTML (HAR, page 4) has 70 result cards of the form `<img class=s-card__image src=…/s-l140.webp data-defer-load=…/s-l500.webp loading=lazy|eager>`, plus a hidden `.s-prefetch-image` div of 10 `s-l500` imgs. No `srcset`, and only 1 `data-src`. eBay's script swaps `src` to the `data-defer-load` URL. The cards had a painted thumbnail, then a `src` that never loads, and the box paints nothing, so the grid goes blank.

### Cause (high confidence)

`Engine::plan_image_loads` (`crates/rustkit-engine/src/lib.rs:18440`, deadline at `:18503`) sets **one wall-clock deadline for the whole plan when the plan is made**: `deadline: self.subresource_deadline()`, which is `now + subresource_budget_ms` (8 s, `lib.rs:18735`). `fetch_planned_images` (`lib.rs:18517`) then runs the plan's **SVG stage first and the raster stage after it**, both against that same deadline (`timeout_at(deadline, …)` at `lib.rs:18558` and `:18644`). A plan added after the load sits in `view.live_images` and is only polled inside `pump_live`'s `settle_live(&mut view.live_images, LIVE_NETWORK_SLICE)` (`lib.rs:6089`, a **2 ms** slice per live turn).

On eBay, the live turns after the load took 1.5, 2.9, 4.4 and 4.7 s (script plus forced layouts). The plan's one SVG started at 18:06:01.24 and was not finished by the 8 s deadline. When it timed out at 18:06:10.43, the 78 rasters started *after* the deadline, and every one failed on its first poll without a request.

Reddit shows the same mechanism (18:01:40 to 18:01:49): 8 images in flight never completed in 8.5 s of 1 to 2.4 s turns, then 11 more failed on first poll. That is 19 dropped.

### Fix (one sentence)

Give each image its own budget that starts when its request actually starts (and not the plan's creation time), run the SVG and raster stages concurrently, and keep painting the element's previous image until the new `src` has decoded (Chrome keeps the old image up during a `src` swap). Of these, LAT9's per-image budget is the core.

### HAR replay (existing tooling, `handtest-runs/hw_job.sh`, headless `cand49`, loopback-only sandbox, 20 s live)

- The swap reproduces: HiWave requests **62 `s-l140` and 64 `s-l500`** images, and the `s-l500` requests are made after scripts run (`Loaded images count=20`, then `added by page scripts count=18`). So the `data-defer-load` to `src` swap is real HiWave behaviour, not something the log implies.
- The budget drop does **not** reproduce headless: 0 `over the subresource budget`. The HAR (Chrome-recorded) lacks 45 `s-l140` and 45 `s-l500` responses, so 90 of the image requests get a replay 404. Headless parity-capture also runs 8 live turns in 20 s, with no 2 ms-slice starvation shaped like the app's content thread. The budget mechanism therefore stands on the live log plus the code reading above. A pin for the fix is an engine test: a live plan whose turns hold longer than `subresource_budget_ms` must still fetch every image.
