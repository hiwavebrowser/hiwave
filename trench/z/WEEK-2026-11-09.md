### Rules for calling a failure root-caused (Pete, 21:50)

- **Watchdog separate from recovery.** First prove the production build arms the 15 s budget, interrupts a pure JavaScript loop, and logs why. Then test recovery separately: a fresh callback runs, the pending request completes, the last good frame stays visible. An interrupt fix gets no credit for recovery until those three pass.
- **i0a_nav judged by what committed.** Cancelled-before-commit navigations correctly leave no visits. The decisive check: the final requested page commits, becomes visible, and is the one in history. Reproduce once without the new-tab shortcut before assigning it to LAT15.
- **Resize measured in two parts.** Native resize acknowledgement and the first correctly sized frame, each on an idle page and under load, before treating the 450–515 ms failures as separate engine bugs.
- The independent reads change the week's order only if they find a blocker or invalidate a measurement.
