## C10(b): rects of transformed boxes read where they paint

PLAN-z row C10, slice (b), off develop `546d6196`. Top of the T13 census's layout list: 44 of 67 sites use `transform`.

Before this, `getBoundingClientRect` and `getClientRects` answered the untransformed box while paint and hit tests moved it. Anything script does with a rect under a transform started from the wrong place: carousels, tooltips, `IntersectionObserver`, `scrollIntoView`.

### Rule, from Chromium 143.0.7499.4 on the same pages
- A rect is the bounding box of the transformed border box, so a rotated box reads wider or taller.
- Transforms multiply down the tree, and each applies about its own origin.
- A scroller's scroll applies inside that scroller's transform: `scrollTop = 10` under `scale(2)` moves a child's rect by 20.
- The window's scroll comes off last.
- `offsetTop`, `offsetLeft`, `offsetWidth`, `offsetHeight`, `client*` and `scroll*` stay where layout put the box.
- A transform does not apply to a plain inline box. It does apply to an inline-block and a flex item.

### How
- `BoxGeometry` gains `transform`, the box's own matrix from `own_transform_affine`, which already mirrors the painter.
- The `scroller` chain now names transformed boxes as well as scrollers, so one walk up the chain keeps them in order. `DomHost::transformed_rect` takes the four corners up it. With no transform on the chain the read is the old path.
- A snapshot is still not rebuilt by a scroll.
- The `geom` string gains the rect's width and height, because `b.w` and `b.h` are `offsetWidth` and `offsetHeight`.

### Changes beyond the row's wording
- **An absolute box under a transformed box that is not positioned now takes that box as its chain parent.** Its rect now follows that box's scroll and transform. The old chain skipped it.
- **Pages will read different numbers.** Every script that reads a rect under a transform now gets Chromium's number. The A/B shows no frame change at scroll 0, but behaviour after interaction is not covered by it.

### Known gaps
- **A fixed box under a transform is placed from the viewport by layout.** It reads `13 24` where Chromium reads `13 124`. This is layout, not the rect read; pinned `#[ignore]` as `a_transform_holds_fixed_boxes`.
- **The painter still transforms a plain inline box**, and so does the hit test, where Chromium does neither. The rect now disagrees with this engine's paint for that one case and agrees with Chromium. Not fixed here.
- **`translate3d` and the other 3D functions were not parsed at all.** That is the next PR, stacked on this one.
- **A transformed element laid out as several boxes** uses its first box's matrix for the union. I know of no page shape that hits this; none is tested.
- **A transformed element is an `offsetParent` in Chromium** (`c10b_probe.mjs`: a child of a static transformed box reads `offsetLeft` from that box). Not here: `offsetParentOf` looks at position only. Belongs with C10(c).
- **Overlap with #850 and #851:** #851 rewrites `element_scroll_over` and the `geom` format, and puts sticky boxes on the same chain. Whichever lands second needs a merge, and `transformed_rect` then needs the sticky shift added to its walk. A sticky box under a transform, or a transformed box inside a sticky one, is untested against #851.
- **No real-window run.** The lane has no input grant, and nothing here changes paint.
