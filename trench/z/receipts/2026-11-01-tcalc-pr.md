## `translate` takes a function with spaces in it

Row C10, found through #851's shopify frame. **#851 should land after this, not before.**

`parse_individual_translate` split the value on all white space, so `translate: 0px calc(-100% - 1px)` made too many parts and the declaration was dropped. Tailwind writes its slide-out panels this way. On shopify each header dropdown has an opaque background and hides by sliding up out of a clipping wrapper. Without the translate, the panel stays where it is.

Develop doesn't show this on shopify, because the whole header sits under the hero. Once #851 paints the sticky header, two black columns cover the hero. With this branch merged into #851 they are gone, checked by eye on one frame at `78fc6298`'s parent build (`2fc4c721`).
