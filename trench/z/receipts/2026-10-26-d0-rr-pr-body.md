## What

A Document can take a new root element once the old one is gone. Before this, `pre_insert` refused every Document parent, so `document.removeChild(document.documentElement); document.appendChild(newHtml)` threw HierarchyRequestError and left the document with no root (and even a comment insert into the Document threw).

- `pre_insert` now accepts a Document parent under DOM §4.2.3 step 6 (`document_child_check`): at most one element child, no text, a doctype only before the element, a fragment with at most one element.
- `replaceChild` / `replaceWith` pass a flag so the "replace" rules apply (the child being taken out is not counted).
