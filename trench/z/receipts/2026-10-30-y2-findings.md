# Y2 yahoo feed, first reduce (Z lane, 2026-10-30 09:05 session, develop 702154ef)

Shape only. No page text or frames are kept here. Load average 6 to 10 during all of it. Each direct or Chrome load is one sample unless a count is given.

## Established

1. **The parsed DOM is not the difference.** The same bytes (the served page with script bodies emptied) in Chrome and the engine give a tree identical in tags, attribute names, attribute value lengths, text lengths and comment shape, once case is ignored: 1400 lines each. Tool: `tools/y2/treecmp.py`.
2. **Chrome logs no #418** under the same user agent (one 30 s load at 1280x800) and fills the feed: at 1280x2400 with no scroll, 22 list items, 19 with links, 16 with images, document height 5795.
3. **#418 is a third-party node in an empty React div.** With a local probe in React's mismatch function (one 800 px run, 34.4 s), the fiber is a `div` with no React children whose only DOM child is `DIV.trc_rbox_container`, inside `DIV#taboola-stream-fp-1`, in `#strm`'s list. Taboola's loader ran at 12.1 s in that run, so the ad script filled the div before React reached it.
4. **#418 does not cause the skeletons in at least one run.** A direct 1280x2400 run, 32 s live: zero #418, feed still on skeleton rows. This answers the T9 "not established" line for that run; it does not exclude #418 making things worse in others.
5. **#418 is intermittent.** Direct 800 px runs: logged in 2 of 3 (23.4 s, 34.4 s; the third was a 3 s live window, too short to count). Direct 2400 px: 0 of 1. Through a pass-through proxy (800 px): 0 of 2. Dev-tools T9 saw it in all of its runs.
6. **The feed's data calls are about 30 s late.** Chrome posts to the media gateway at 2.6 s. The engine (the 2400 px run) posts at 32.4 s, gets 200s by 34.5 to 36.5 s, and the capture ended at 37.9 s. Chrome made 14 of these calls in 30 s, the engine 12 in 37.9 s.

7. **The rows do not fill after the gateway answers.** A direct 1280x2400 run, 70 s live, ended at 75.3 s: zero #418, gateway posted at 29.9 s (9 calls) and 60.9 s (3 calls), feed still on skeleton rows. Same at 32 s in point 4. So neither #418 nor the gateway's lateness alone explains the skeletons.
8. **The page is mostly idle.** In that run, script time totals about 6 s of 75 s; the longest single script is 1.2 s. Slow execution does not account for a 30 s delay.

## Not established

- **What the 30 s spacing is.** 29.9 s and 60.9 s (32.4 s and 35.1 s in the shorter run, so not fixed) look like a timer or fallback, where Chrome's call comes at 2.6 s from some other trigger. This is a reading of two runs, not a finding: the initiator field only says `fetch`.
- **Whether the gateway is the feed's data source at all.** Nothing links its answer to the rows. T9 gap 8 is the same hole.
- The machine was loaded (6 to 10) throughout; no quiet-hour timing.

## Separate defects seen, not worked (no row exists)

- **SVG element case:** `svg.tagName` reads `SVG`, `PATH`, `G` in the engine; Chrome keeps lowercase in the SVG namespace.
- **SVG attribute case:** the parser keeps `viewbox` and `preserveaspectratio` lowercase; Chrome adjusts to `viewBox` and `preserveAspectRatio`.
- **`template.content`:** on 2 of the page's `<template>` lines the dump's `n.content.childNodes` branch printed `[content 0]` in the engine and nothing in Chrome. Unread, may be a tool artifact.
- **Overlap at 1280x2400:** the footer and "Advertisement" labels paint over the feed, and the hero and row content run past the right edge (T9 gap 6 also saw this).

## Tools
