## C10: which depths a 3D transform takes

Batch-testing trial. PLAN-z C10, the two items from #853's R1. **Full gates in batch:** suites, campaign and 20-site A/B are left to the batch build. Parse only: a depth is still flattened and not kept.

### Rule, from Chromium 143.0.7499.4
- **A percentage anywhere in a depth's `calc()` refuses the transform,** for `translateZ`, the z of `translate3d`, and `perspective`, even when it sums to nothing: `calc(5% - 5%)`, `calc(0% + 0px)`. A `calc()` that comes to a bare number refuses it too: `translateZ(calc(1))`.
- **A plain negative `perspective` is refused by its sign, in any unit:** `-1vw`, `-1vh`, `-1vmin`, `-1vmax`, `-1em`, `-1rem`. The order asked to resolve vw and vh against the real viewport; Chromium's answer doesn't depend on it, so this reads the sign and needs no viewport.
- **A `calc()` is never refused for its sign:** `perspective(calc(1vw - 100px))` and `calc(-1px)` are taken and clamp to zero when used.

Before: `translateZ(calc(10%))` and `perspective(-1vw)` were taken as valid. A refused value drops the whole `transform`, so a page writing one had a stacking context and a matrix that Chromium does not have.

- Red `2e6e11bc`: `transform_parse_tests` 25 passed, 2 failed of the 27 run (`a_depth_refuses_a_percentage_inside_calc`, `a_negative_perspective_is_refused_in_any_unit`). The two other new tests are controls for what must stay valid, and passed before and after.

### Not in this PR

- `-0px` for `perspective` was not probed. It is taken, as before.
