## C10(b2): the 3D transform functions parse; a bad value refuses the declaration

PLAN-z row C10, follow-on to slice (b), found by its red tests. **Stacked on #852 (C10(b)):** off its head `9551f252`, and measured against it, because the tests read rects. The diff against develop includes that PR until it lands.

**Not on the row by name; Atlas, please place it or send it back.** I took it because `transform: translate3d(...)` is a common idiom and does nothing on develop.

Before this, `parse_transform` skipped any function it did not know:
- `translate3d`, `translateZ`, `scale3d`, `scaleZ`, `rotateZ`, `rotateX`, `rotateY`, `rotate3d`, `matrix3d` and `perspective` did nothing, so `translate3d(-50%,0,0)` left the box where it was and `translateZ(0)` made no transform.
- `translateX(5px) nonsense(1)` kept its first half, where Chromium refuses the declaration.
- Function names were case-sensitive, `scale(50%)` was refused, and `translate(5px 6px)`, `translate(5,6)` and `rotate(90)` were accepted.

### Rule, from Chromium 143.0.7499.4 (`trench/z/tools/c10/c10b2_probe.mjs`)
- With no perspective a 3D function flattens: it keeps what it does to x and y. `rotateX(60deg)` halves the height and `rotateY(60deg)` the width. `matrix3d` keeps `m11 m12 m21 m22 m41 m42`.
- `translateZ`, `scaleZ` and `perspective()` move nothing on the page's plane, but they are transforms. They still make a containing block and a stacking context, as `translateZ(0)` does in Chromium.
- Argument counts are exact and commas are required. A percentage depth is refused. A bare number is a length or an angle only when it is zero.

### Changes beyond the row's wording
- **`translateZ(0)` and `translate3d(0,0,0)` now create a real transform on every page that uses them as a GPU hint.** The box becomes a containing block for its fixed and absolute descendants and paints through `PushTransform`. Chromium does the same, but it exposes the fixed-box gap below on more pages, and it was not timed.
- **Stricter parsing can remove a transform that used to apply**, on pages with a value Chromium also refuses.
- `perspective(none)` is accepted, as in Chromium.

### Known gaps
- **Perspective with depth is lost:** `perspective(500px) translateZ(100px)` should scale to 125x62.5 and does nothing. The painter holds a 2D affine. Pinned `#[ignore]` as `perspective_with_depth`.
- **`em`, `rem`, `vw` and `vh` inside a transform are wrong, on develop too.** `to_matrix` resolves against a fixed 16px and a zero viewport, so `translate(1vw,1vh)` does nothing and `1em` is always 16px. A fix needs those resolved where the element's font and the viewport are known. Pinned `#[ignore]` as `font_and_viewport_units_in_a_translate`.
- **A fixed box under a transform is placed from the viewport** (from #852), now reachable through `translateZ(0)`.
- `preserve-3d` and `backface-visibility` are not modelled, and nothing composes depth between boxes.
- The individual `translate`, `rotate` and `scale` properties keep their own parsers, which drop a nonzero z or another axis. That differs from what this PR does for `transform`, and Chromium keeps them.
- `rotate3d`'s and `matrix3d`'s `w` other than 1 are not divided through.
- **No real-window run.** The lane has no input grant. Worth trying: a carousel or drawer that animates by `translate3d`, once script changes the value.
