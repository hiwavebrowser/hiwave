## fix: `display: inherit` copies the parent's display, `contents` included

`inherit_property` had no `display` arm, so `display: inherit` kept what the element already had and a `<div>` stayed `block` whatever its parent was. Found while reducing apple's global nav for #786. The nav's two inner wrappers say `display: inherit` under a `display: contents` menu item of a flex list. In Chromium all three compute `contents`, and the items are flex items of the list.

This head (`ec8ee96c`) has develop `c25c1d71` merged, with #786 on it, so the follow-up the first version of this PR left open is now here.

### Change
- `3baab71a` (red), `1db1c666`: the arm copies the parent's computed `display`, its legacy `-webkit-box` and `display_contents_declared`.
- `b1cc2de8` (red, 3 of 5 `display_inherit_tests` failing on this branch with develop merged): `display: inherit` under a `contents` parent read the grandparent's value, because #786 hands a contents box's children a parent style with `display` replaced, so that they blockify correctly.
- `ec8ee96c`: the arm answers `contents` when `parent.display_contents_declared` is set. This is Prometheus's F1 on #786, from the saved 10-28 patch.
