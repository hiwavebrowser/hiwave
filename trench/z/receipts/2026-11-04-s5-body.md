## S5: visually hidden ("screen-reader-only") boxes stop painting

PLAN-z row **S5** (Pete's hand test, eBay HAR, Grok triage 11-03): eBay's `.clipped` header labels ("expand Cart") paint as visible boxes. The visually-hidden pattern is on most large sites.

**Causes (two):**
1. The legacy `clip` property was not parsed at all, so `clip: rect(0,0,0,0)` / `rect(1px,1px,1px,1px)` (Bootstrap `.visually-hidden`, `.sr-only` and many more) did nothing, and the text overflowing a 1x1 box without `overflow:hidden` painted.
2. `clip-path` was only applied to block and image boxes, so on a `<button>` or `<input>` (eBay's header "expand" buttons) `clip-path: inset(50%)` did nothing either.

**Fix:**
- `rustkit-css`: `ComputedStyle::clip: Option<ClipRect>` + `parse_clip` (comma or space form, `auto` sides; refuses percentages, bare non-zero numbers, wrong counts, as Chromium's `CSS.supports` does). Not inherited; `initial` resets it.
- engine: `clip` cascade arm.
- `rustkit-layout`: `clip_rect_clip` (CSS 2.1 §11.1.2): an absolutely or fixed positioned block, image or form control pushes a clip for its box and subtree, offsets from its border-box corner, `auto` = that border edge; static/relative boxes ignore it. Pushed just outside the `clip-path` clip, popped after it. `clip_path_clip` now also takes `BoxType::FormControl`.
