CSS Flexbox §4.3 and Grid §9.5 say a flex or grid item whose `z-index` is not `auto` paints as if it were `position: relative`. RustKit put the item in normal flow, under the collage. Today develop hides this because it never shows the collage (HT27 N1, `atlas/z-ht27-n1-abspos-flex`, held for this fix).

- Floats keep their z 0 sort key.
