## CA1-1: `:is()` compound members beside a complex one read attributes and `:not()`

Castor's A1 audit of #822 (PLAN-z row CA1, item 1). Left of a combinator, an `:is()`/`:where()` with a complex member (`.dark *`) is decided by `AncestorCompound::complex_alternatives_hold` against the ancestor's chain. Its **compound** members were read through `matches()`, which looks only at tag, classes and id. So a member that is only an attribute selector or a `:not()` matched every ancestor.

Theme toggles have exactly this shape: `:is([data-theme="dark"], .dark *) > .child` painted the dark theme under `data-theme="light"`.

### Fix
- `matches_ancestor` becomes a thin wrapper over the new `matches_carried(tag, classes, id, attributes)`. Nothing about its behaviour changes.
- `complex_alternatives_hold` now runs its compound members through `matches_carried` with the attributes the element carries. Where attributes are carried, `[...]`, `:not()` and nested `:is()` compounds are decided. Where they are not carried (the sibling path), these parts stay permissive, the same as the rest of the ancestor path.
